Our Story

Built on conviction,
grown through proximity.

Prime Equity Group was founded by two partners who arrived in Bali nearly a decade ago — and never left. What began as a personal decision to plant roots in one of the world's most dynamic markets evolved into a structured approach to identifying, acquiring, and holding premium Balinese real estate.

Nick and Riley bring complementary perspectives to every opportunity: an eye for emerging value, a deep network built over nine years on the ground, and the operational infrastructure of a licensed PMA to move with speed and confidence.

We invest with patience. We hold with conviction. And we know this island in a way that takes years to earn.

"We didn't move to Bali to invest in real estate. We moved to Bali — and investing in it was the obvious next step."

Nick & Riley Co-Founders, Prime Equity Group
