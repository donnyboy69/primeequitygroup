Common Questions

What you need to know.

A PMA (Penanaman Modal Asing) is a foreign-owned limited liability company registered in Indonesia. It's the legal structure that allows foreigners to own and operate businesses - including holding real estate assets - directly in Indonesia. Prime Equity Group operates as a fully licensed PT PMA.

Foreigners cannot hold freehold title (Hak Milik) personally. However, through a PMA structure, you can acquire land under Hak Pakai (Right to Use) or Hak Guna Bangunan (Right to Build) - both of which are legally recognized and renewable. Leasehold agreements are also common and offer long-term control of land, typically 25-30 years with extension options.

Freehold (Hak Milik) is permanent ownership held by an Indonesian citizen or entity. When we say "freehold" in our listings, it means the land carries a freehold certificate - the strongest title available. Leasehold is a contractual right to use land for a fixed period (usually 25-30 years), renewable by agreement. Both are legitimate paths depending on your goals and timeline.

South Bali (Canggu, Seminyak, Uluwatu) is largely built out. Land prices are 5-10x what they are in Medewi, and the congestion, overdevelopment, and zoning restrictions make new projects harder. Medewi offers ocean-front land at early-stage pricing with clear growth catalysts: new toll road access, government tourism development plans, and a wave of boutique hospitality moving west.

Once you've identified a property, we handle due diligence on title, zoning, and land status. From there, a notary (PPAT) facilitates the legal transfer. For PMA purchases, we coordinate with legal counsel to ensure the structure is compliant. The process typically takes 4-8 weeks from agreement to completion. We're hands-on through every step.
